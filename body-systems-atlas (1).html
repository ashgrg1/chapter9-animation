Part 4 summary

When something goes wrong, in one line each

PhysiologyHow the body works. Anatomy is the photo, physiology is the movie.
HomeostasisEverything in balance. Disrupted balance = disease.
Causes of diseasePoisons, germs, lack of nutrients or oxygen, genetics, aging, unknown.
PathophysiologyThe study of what goes wrong in disease.
RolesDoctor diagnoses. Pharmacist is the treatment expert. Tech refers OTC symptom questions to the pharmacist.
Risk factorsRaise disease chances: family history, relatives, inactivity, age.
PharmacogenomicsGenes guide drug and dose. Fewer liver enzymes → slow breakdown → lower dose.
Signs vs symptomsTextbook: sign = subjective, symptom = objective. Standard: symptom = felt, sign = measured.
EpidemiologyStudies large groups. Compare who ate what to find the bad dish.
4 treatmentsLifestyle, reduce risk factors, surgery, pharmacotherapy (medicines).
